Our Story

Simply FYN

“We couldn’t find a real New York cookie in Lebanon — so we made our own.”

FYN started with one obsession: the fat, gooey, stuffed New York–style cookie — the kind that pulls apart with a molten center and ruins every other cookie for you. We baked, we tested, we fed the harshest critics we know (our friends), and we didn’t stop until every batch hit.

Everything is baked fresh to order, in small batches — because “for those who value quality” isn’t just a bio line, it’s the whole point. Reserve a box, and we bake it for you.

— the FYN kitchen
